Invisible AI

Production

Fireart Studio

Year

2024

Scope of Work

Animation, Modeling, Lookdev, Compositing

My first project where I assembled most of some of the scenes in 3d. On this project I first got acquainted with the specifics of toon shading. Also there were quite a lot of difficulties with understanding of 3D rendering. I met AOVs and multi passes for the first time. But in the end all the scenes turned out to be assembled very close to stilframes and on compose there were no problems with the composing of the final image.
