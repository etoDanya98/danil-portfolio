Passion for CG: From Graphic Designer to CG Generalist, My Journey So Far.

I am self-taught. I started to be interested in computer graphics in my school years and discovered Photoshop for the first time. For a long time I have been learning new software, exploring new techniques for working with graphics, taking various courses and finding mentors.

Today I have good animation skills and can achieve expressive dynamics in any software. I am able to build complex full CG scenes from the sketch stage to the final compositing stage. But in addition to working with visuals, I love to organize, optimize and automate my workflows. I also firmly believe that CG is a team game and I am always ready to fight for my team's goals.
